About PixSafe

PixSafe is a web-based platform designed to help users check whether a file or link is safe or harmful before interacting with it.

In today’s digital world, users often open unknown links or download files without verification, which can lead to cyber threats like phishing and malware.

Our system allows users to upload an image or enter a URL, and instantly analyzes it to detect potential risks.

The goal of PixSafe is to provide a simple, fast, and user-friendly solution to improve online safety and awareness.
